I love this idea. Yes, as the author admits, it's basically just *parenting*. But giving it a name and making it a tradition changes it. Ordinary time becomes something you both look forward to, and it's special for the parent and the kid.

We don't do *Dad-urday*, but I have my own rituals that have deepened my relationship with my kids, like taking them to school every day. What matters is finding consistent ways to connect with your children, starting when they're young.
