I finally sat down to add new photos to my [photo site](https://photos.reubeningber.com), and I realized it had gotten away from me.

Family was one long scroll. Eighty-one photos, in no order I could explain. Little League was filed under Events, right next to Manhattanhenge. Click on Travel and you got a page that didn't exist.

It needed a cleanup. Mostly because I'm shooting more than I used to.

This fall I started going to photo events. A car meet on the cobblestones in SoHo. From Fear to Flash with B&H on the High Line. And the one that got me: Adorama's shoot in the Flower District.

That was my first time working with pro lighting and real models. I felt like a real photographer. The photos came out looking like they belonged in a magazine.

The hobby really came alive that day.

My favorite frame from the shoot is a model in a white top against a wall of red flowers, chin in her hand. It's not the album cover. The cover is the smoking man in the tux, mostly because he fit the layout better.

So I rebuilt the site.

Every section has its own page now, with a cover for each album. The events have room to grow. Family is split by year and then by season. Little League moved out of Events and into Family, where it always belonged. And the homepage is made up of photos I tag as favorites inside each album. I mark one once, and it shows up in both places.

But the part I care about most is Family.

I have thousands of photos of my kids. I'm biased, but they're the cutest. The site isn't all of them. It's the ones I picked.

Now there's a place to come see them, one season at a time.
