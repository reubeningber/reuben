I've been on the engineering team at Business Insider for over 10 years.

This week, my photos ran on the site.

[![Business Insider's photo story on the 2026 NYC Marathon, with my photo of the elite men's field as the lead image](https://res.cloudinary.com/dt3vcpkj6/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto,w_1400/v1793753317/web_assets/bi-marathon-browser-window-no-ad.png)](https://www.businessinsider.com/see-best-photos-2026-nyc-marathon-celebrities-2026-11)

Marathon Sunday starts for me on the Whitehall Street subway platform, next to someone in a red velvet robe. Outside the ferry, a woman is selling glitter. On the plaza, a couple kisses goodbye like one of them is shipping out instead of heading to Staten Island.

Later I'm at East 60th Street when the men's pro field comes through. One of the runners in my frame is Andreas Almgren. He goes on to win the whole thing.

That's the shot Business Insider used to lead [Tristin Hoffman's story](https://www.businessinsider.com/see-best-photos-2026-nyc-marathon-celebrities-2026-11).

The rest of the day is [in the album](https://photos.reubeningber.com/running/tcs-nyc-marathon-2026/), all 52 photos of it, including a sign I found on the Upper East Side:

"You will finish. Why would I deceive you?"

I ran this race in 2014. I finished. Nobody deceived me.
